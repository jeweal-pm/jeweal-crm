# GIS247 Retail Workflow Journey

## Funnel purpose

Help jewelry retailers connect daily store work into one practical workflow: POS, customer history, scheduling, task coordination, and multi-branch visibility.

## Audience and trigger

- Trigger: a GIS247 enquiry where the contact may be evaluating retail operations, POS, or customer management.
- Audience: jewelry retailers, store managers, sales leaders, and growing multi-branch teams.
- Stop when the contact replies, requests a demo or quotation, signs up, unsubscribes, or withdraws consent.

## Content journey

| Step | Timing | Marketing job | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately | Confirm the enquiry and ask for the operational priority | Tell us the workflow |
| 2 | Day 1 | Show how POS supports everyday selling | Explore GIS247 POS |
| 3 | Day 3 | Explain customer history and follow-up context | Explore Customer Profile |
| 4 | Day 5 | Connect Calendar and KANBAN to team execution | See the team workflow |
| 5 | Day 7 | Invite a focused conversation and close with permission | Plan a retail workflow review |

## Stop and branch rules

- Stop the sequence when the reader replies, requests a demo, asks for a quotation, signs up, unsubscribes, or withdraws consent.
- POS click: ask about store devices, checkout speed, or branch consistency.
- Customer Profile click: ask about clienteling, purchase history, or follow-up.
- Calendar/KANBAN click: ask about tasks, appointments, or team coordination.

## Messaging principles

1. Keep each email focused on one retail workflow problem.
2. Use practical language instead of broad software claims.
3. Link named features to the matching GIS247 page.
4. Include useful inline links, one clear CTA button, a reply fallback, and unsubscribe in every step.
5. The matching HTML files are body fragments; the CRM adds the GIS247 branded shell.
