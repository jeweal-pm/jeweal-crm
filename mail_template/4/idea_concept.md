# GIS247 AI and Growth Journey

## Funnel purpose

Introduce GIS247 as a practical way to connect intelligent search, store operations, and digital growth. The sequence moves from curiosity to one relevant action without assuming the reader is ready for a full platform decision.

## Audience and trigger

- Trigger: a GIS247 enquiry or marketing-consented contact interested in improving retail technology or growth.
- Audience: jewelry business owners, digital leads, and teams exploring AI, POS, e-commerce, or connected operations.
- Stop when the contact replies, requests a demo or quotation, signs up, unsubscribes, or withdraws consent.

## Content journey

| Step | Timing | Marketing job | Primary CTA |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Immediately | Confirm interest and ask what growth problem matters | Describe the priority |
| 2 | Day 1 | Make AI useful through a concrete search workflow | Explore Faro AI |
| 3 | Day 3 | Connect digital intelligence to store selling | Explore POS |
| 4 | Day 5 | Show how connected tools support online and multi-channel growth | Explore GIS247 features |
| 5 | Day 7 | Invite a signup or focused conversation | Start the GIS247 journey |

## Stop and branch rules

- Stop after a reply, demo request, quotation request, signup, unsubscribe, or consent withdrawal.
- Faro AI click: follow up on search, image, or product discovery needs.
- POS click: ask about store devices, checkout, and branch operations.
- Signup click: notify the sales owner and create a human follow-up task.

## Messaging principles

1. Explain AI through a business task rather than a technical claim.
2. Make every email useful even if the reader does not click.
3. Link named features to official GIS247 feature pages.
4. Use one primary CTA, useful inline links, a reply fallback, and unsubscribe in every step.
5. The matching HTML files are body fragments; the CRM adds the GIS247 branded shell.
